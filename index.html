Who am I

A little about me.

Hi! I'm Jade — a high school student with a deep love for science, asking big questions, and finding elegant answers. Whether it's dissecting a biology concept or building a project from scratch, I'm always looking for ways to understand the world more deeply.

Outside the classroom, you'll find me writing analytical articles, running experiments, and exploring how scientific ideas connect to everyday life.

This portfolio is a living document of my growth — the projects I've built, the ideas I've explored, and the writing that has sharpened my thinking.
